How the numbers are calculated. "Monthly avg" is a rolling 30-day average and "Yearly avg" a rolling 365-day average, both recalculated for every day with data (trailing calendar windows, so gaps in the data don't distort them). "vs. last month" compares today's 30-day average with the 30-day average 30 days earlier; "vs. last year" compares today's 365-day average with the one 365 days earlier. Changes smaller than a metric's normal day-to-day noise are shown as "stable".

Sources in the data. Apple Watch readings are preferred; older history comes from Fitbit (via sync apps). Steps and exercise minutes use the device with the highest daily total to avoid double counting phone and watch. Implausible values (sync glitches) are filtered out.

This dashboard is for personal tracking and is not medical advice. Consumer wearables estimate these values, and individual readings can be noisy — the rolling averages are the more reliable signal. Discuss any concerning trends with your doctor.
